Digital IC Design 2026

SRAM-Based 4-bit MAC Accelerator

A custom 6T SRAM bitcell, DRC/LVS clean and characterized post-layout, tiled into a 16 × 4 macro and built into a pipelined 4-bit MAC that closes at 0.90 ns at 0.39 pJ per multiply-accumulate. ESE 5700, with Dhruva Jindal.

Analog PCB 2026

Metal Detector PCB

An LC-oscillator detector for ESE 3190, routed as a two-layer board, soldered by hand, then debugged stage by stage: the oscillator landed at 50.2 kHz against 50.27 kHz in LTspice.

PCB Layout Analog Circuits Soldering Testing
Bioelectronics 2026

Wireless ECG Monitor

A multi-stage analog front end for ESE 2150 — AD623 instrumentation amp, virtual ground, 0.5 Hz high-pass and 160 Hz low-pass — sampled by a Feather and streamed over BLE, with Python finding R-peaks on the receiving end. Measured 61 dB maximum gain and a −3 dB corner at 190.5 Hz.

ECG Analog Filters Feather Python
Embedded Audio 2026

Guitar Hero Synthesizer

A three-person ESE 3500 build: a Guitar Hero controller rebuilt around an ATmega328PB, with bare-metal C firmware driving PWM audio, an I2C LCD for remapping fret notes, and a 12 V pack split across two 5 V LDOs after the speaker kept shutting the board down.

ATmega328PB Bare-Metal C PWM Audio I2C

A three-person ESE 1110 prototype: a trail-mounted node with an 8 × 8 LED matrix, temperature and CO₂ sensing, a distress button, XBee links that meshed in testing, and a solar supply, inside a CAD enclosure I designed.
