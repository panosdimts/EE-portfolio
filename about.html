About

Hardware, circuits, and measured systems.

I am Panagiotis Dimtsoudis, an Electrical Engineering student at Penn Engineering. I grew up on a farm in northeastern Greece, where repairing equipment and working with physical systems were part of daily life. That background still shapes how I approach circuits, boards, instruments, and embedded projects.

Engineering Interests

Main technical interests.

PCB and embedded hardware

I like projects where the electrical design must work after assembly, wiring, measurement noise, and debugging. My course and personal work spans custom boards, analog front ends, microcontroller firmware, and hardware integration.

Device characterization and fabrication

At Penn's Device Research and Engineering Laboratory, I support electrical characterization of AlScN ferroelectric junctions through measurement organization, data analysis, and device visualization.

Current direction

I am interested in compact electronics, IC design, fabrication, and measurement-driven development, while continuing to build hardware systems that can be tested and explained clearly.
