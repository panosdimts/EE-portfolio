Digital IC Design2026

SRAM-Based 4-bit MAC Accelerator

A 0.53 µm² 6T SRAM bitcell with 0.13 V post-layout read SNM, tiled into a 16 × 4 macro and built into a pipelined 4-bit MAC that closes at 0.90 ns and costs 0.39 pJ per multiply-accumulate. ESE 5700, with Dhruva Jindal.

Analog PCB2026

Metal Detector PCB

A variable-inductance LC-oscillator detector: schematic, two-layer layout, fabricated board, hand-soldered and debugged stage by stage. The oscillator came in at 50.2 kHz against 50.27 kHz in LTspice.

Bioelectronics2026

Wireless ECG Monitor

An ECG front end: AD623 instrumentation amp, 0.5 Hz high-pass and 160 Hz low-pass, streamed over BLE at 200 Hz. Measured 61 dB maximum gain, with the −3 dB corner at 190.5 Hz against that 160 Hz target.

Embedded Audio2026

Guitar Hero Synthesizer

A Guitar Hero controller rebuilt into an ATmega328PB synthesizer: bare-metal C, PWM audio with a decay envelope, whammy-position vibrato read through the ADC, and an I2C LCD for remapping fret notes.

Embedded Systems2024

EcoBeam

A solar-powered trail node, three-person team: 8 × 8 LED matrix, temperature and CO₂ sensing, a distress button, and XBee radios that meshed between nodes in testing.
