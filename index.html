  2. SRAM-Based 4‑bit MAC Accelerator

    • Digital IC design
    • ESE 5700
    • 2026
    • With Dhruva Jindal
    Complete

    The core operation of a neural-network accelerator, a multiply-accumulate fed by two custom SRAMs, designed from the 6T bitcell up in Cadence Virtuoso.

    • Read margin (SNM)0.13 V (16% of the 0.8 V supply), post-layout
    • Macro access440 ps simulated, 16 × 4 macro
    • Minimum clock0.90 ns on the specified test vectors
    • Energy0.39 pJ per multiply-accumulate, simulated, clock excluded

  3. Metal Detector PCB

    • Analog PCB
    • ESE 3190
    • 2026
    • Solo
    Complete

    Two MOSFET LC oscillators beat against each other, and metal near the search coil shifts the tone. I took it from schematic to a fabricated two-layer board, soldered it by hand, and brought it up stage by stage.

    • Fixed oscillatorLTspice predicted 50.27 kHz measured result 50.2 kHz

  4. Wireless ECG Monitor

    • Bioelectronics
    • ESE 2150
    • 2025
    • With Damodar Pai
    Complete

    An analog front end that lifts a heartbeat of about a millivolt out of electrode drift and 60 Hz pickup, then streams it over Bluetooth Low Energy at 200 samples per second.

    • Maximum gain61 dB, measured
    • Low-pass cornertarget predicted 160 Hz measured result 190.5 Hz

  5. Guitar Hero Synthesizer

    • Embedded audio
    • ESE 3500
    • 2026
    • Team of three
    Complete

    A Guitar Hero controller rebuilt as a playable synthesizer on an ATmega328PB: bare-metal C, PWM audio with a decay envelope, whammy vibrato read through the ADC, and an I2C LCD for remapping fret notes.

    • What brokeThe board reset whenever the speaker drew current. A 12 V pack and two 5 V regulators fixed it.

    • 2024
    • Team of three
